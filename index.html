Data integrity boundary

All supplied Task 01 records are SIMULATED / FICTIONAL. The reported percentages describe only the exercise sample, not actual VELOOP users, revenue or product performance. Snapshot activity flags are not event-derived DAU/MAU or retention. Referral acquisition is not referral conversion. Transactional and time-series measures need the relevant history tables.

The original user-level source workbook and profiling workbook are intentionally not published because they contain name/email-style fields. Aggregate metrics and reproducible methodology are public. Proposed tracking, data architecture, privacy controls and dashboards are not claims of production implementation.
